# 🎮 KALKE THEKE SERIOUS
**Genre:** 2D Comedy / Maze Escape  
**Theme:** Bouncing Back  
**Game Engine:** Godot 4

### Game Description

**KALKE THEKE SERIOUS** is a funny, chaotic 2D maze game inspired by the everyday struggles of a Bangladeshi student who has an exam tomorrow but absolutely refuses to study today.

The player controls a schoolboy trapped inside a maze-like bedroom filled with study tables, textbooks, schoolbags, and other educational obstacles. His only mission? **Reach his phone on the other side of the maze without studying or getting caught by his mom!**

But there's a catch. Every time the boy approaches a study table or touches an educational object, an invisible force dramatically **bounces him backward**, blocking his path and forcing him to find another route.

As if avoiding books wasn't difficult enough, **random mom characters patrol the maze**, searching for their procrastinating son. If a mom catches him, it's an instant **GAME OVER!**

The player must navigate through narrow pathways, avoid bouncing obstacles, dodge patrolling moms, and discover the correct route to reach the phone.

### Core Features
- **Maze Exploration:** Navigate a colorful, top-down 2D maze designed like a chaotic student's bedroom.
- **Bounce-Back Obstacles:** Study tables, books, and schoolbags repel the player, sending him backward whenever he gets too close.
- **Mom Patrols:** Funny mom avatars wander through the maze. Getting caught means losing the game.
- **Stealth & Strategy:** Observe mom movements, avoid blocked paths, and find safe routes.
- **Funny Banglish Reactions:** "Porte boshbo na!", "Ma ashtese!", "Ajke mood nai!", and "Kalke theke serious, pakka!"
- **The Final Goal:** Successfully reach the phone and escape academic responsibilities.
